## 💡 Tips for Success

### Do:
- ✅ Be honest about your age
- ✅ Be professional

- ✅ Personalize each email
- ✅ Keep it concise

- ❌ Oversell
- ❌ Hide your age
- ❌ Send generic emails
- ❌ Give up after one try
